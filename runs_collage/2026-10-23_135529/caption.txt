TITLE: the 5 apps my whole business runs on

the only 5 apps I'd keep if I had to start over tomorrow

2. checkvibe.dev: a security scanner for websites, paste a link, get a report of what to fix in 30 seconds
3. Antigravity: Google's AI builder, I give it a job and it plans and does it by itself
4. slideys.app: the app that makes these exact slideshows, describe your apps and it builds the whole post for you
5. Lovable: an AI app builder, describe an app in a sentence and it builds the whole thing for you
6. Notion: a notes and planning app, every plan, idea and to-do in one place

I used to spend weeks hunting for the 'perfect tool.' now I just build. these five let me do that without thinking about my setup. if you've got an idea you keep putting off, this is your sign.
