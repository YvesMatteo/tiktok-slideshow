TITLE: 5 apps every solo founder needs

things i wish i knew before starting a business at 18: the tools

1. Higgsfield: an AI that makes my photos, ads and videos, no camera or designer needed
2. checkvibe.dev: a security scanner for websites, paste a link, get a report of what to fix in 30 seconds
3. Notion: a notes and planning app, every plan, idea and to-do in one place
4. Obsidian: a private notes app, everything saved on my own computer forever
5. slideys.app: the app that makes these exact slideshows, describe your apps and it builds the whole post for you
6. Lovable: an AI app builder, describe an app in a sentence and it builds the whole thing for you

learn these and you're ahead of basically everyone still 'researching.' the goal isn't more tools, it's shipping something real.
