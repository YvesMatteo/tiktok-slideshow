TITLE: 5 apps that run my entire business

CAPTION:
things i wish i knew before starting a business at 18: the tools

i wasted months on the wrong apps. here's the short list that actually matters:

1. Notion: a notes and planning app, every plan, idea and to-do in one place
2. checkvibe.dev: a security scanner for websites, paste a link, get a report of what to fix in 30 seconds
3. Supabase: a database for apps, stores all my data and logins, the whole engine room in one place
4. GitHub: where builders store their code, it saves every version of my work so I can never lose anything
5. Claude: an AI assistant I think through ideas with, it even helps me build my apps
6. slideys.app: the app that makes these exact slideshows, describe your apps and it builds the whole post for you

learn these and you're ahead of basically everyone still 'researching.' the goal isn't more tools, it's shipping something real.
