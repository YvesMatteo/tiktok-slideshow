TITLE: the 5 apps my whole business runs on

CAPTION:
the only 5 apps I'd keep if I had to start over tomorrow

after two years of building apps on my own, I've cut everything down to what actually matters. every app on this list earns its spot:

1. Notion: a notes and planning app, every plan, idea and to-do in one place
2. checkvibe.dev: a security scanner for websites, paste a link, get a report of what to fix in 30 seconds
3. Higgsfield: an AI that makes my photos, ads and videos, no camera or designer needed
4. Supabase: a database for apps, stores all my data and logins, the whole engine room in one place
5. Claude: an AI assistant I think through ideas with, it even helps me build my apps
6. Obsidian: a private notes app, everything saved on my own computer forever

I used to spend weeks hunting for the 'perfect tool.' now I just build. these five let me do that without thinking about my setup. if you've got an idea you keep putting off, this is your sign.

save this and stop researching. you don't need more tools, you need to start. follow for the unfiltered version of building a business alone.
