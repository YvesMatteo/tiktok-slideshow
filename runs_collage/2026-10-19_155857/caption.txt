I quit my 9-5 to build apps full time. two years of trying every tool later, these are the only 5 I actually open every day:

1. Higgsfield: an AI that makes my photos, ads and videos, no camera or designer needed
2. checkvibe.dev: a security scanner for websites, paste a link, get a report of what to fix in 30 seconds
3. GitHub: where builders store their code, it saves every version of my work so I can never lose anything
4. PostHog: an analytics tool that shows me exactly what people do in my app, every click, every exit
5. Lovable: an AI app builder, describe an app in a sentence and it builds the whole thing for you
6. Obsidian: a private notes app, everything saved on my own computer forever

no team, no office, no investors. just me, my laptop and these 5 apps. if you've been wanting to build something of your own, an app, a side hustle, anything, this is the exact setup I'd copy.
