TITLE: 5 apps every solo founder needs

CAPTION:
i'm 18 and run a business from my laptop. these 5 apps are how 👇

no degree, no team, no office. just me and a few apps that do the heavy lifting:

1. Framer: a no-code website builder, design by dragging things around, hit publish and it's live
2. checkvibe.dev: a security scanner for websites, paste a link, get a report of what to fix in 30 seconds
3. Supabase: a database for apps, stores all my data and logins, the whole engine room in one place
4. Notion: a notes and planning app, every plan, idea and to-do in one place
5. Vercel: a hosting service, it puts my apps live on the internet with one click
6. slideys.app: the app that makes these exact slideshows, describe your apps and it builds the whole post for you

if you're young and want to build something instead of waiting around, this is the exact stack i'd start with. you don't need money, you need to start.
