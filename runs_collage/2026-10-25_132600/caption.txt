TITLE: 5 apps that run my entire business

how i build a whole app in a weekend (the tools)

people think it takes a team. it takes these five apps and a free weekend:

3. Vercel: a hosting service, it puts my apps live on the internet with one click
4. Antigravity: Google's AI builder, I give it a job and it plans and does it by itself
5. Claude: an AI assistant I think through ideas with, it even helps me build my apps
6. GitHub: where builders store their code, it saves every version of my work so I can never lose anything

idea on friday, live by sunday. that's the loop, and these are the tools that make it possible solo.
