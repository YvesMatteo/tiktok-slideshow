TITLE: the 5 apps my whole business runs on

CAPTION:
how i build a whole app in a weekend (the tools)

people think it takes a team. it takes these five apps and a free weekend:

1. Framer: a no-code website builder, design by dragging things around, hit publish and it's live
2. checkvibe.dev: a security scanner for websites, paste a link, get a report of what to fix in 30 seconds
3. Railway: a hosting service that keeps my apps' behind-the-scenes machinery running, zero headaches
4. PostHog: an analytics tool that shows me exactly what people do in my app, every click, every exit
5. GitHub: where builders store their code, it saves every version of my work so I can never lose anything
6. Higgsfield: an AI that makes my photos, ads and videos, no camera or designer needed

idea on friday, live by sunday. that's the loop, and these are the tools that make it possible solo.
